title: "Where your customers get lost after they click your ad"
description: "A simple walk through the journey from ad to payment, and the most common places interested people drop off."
date: 2026-11-16
---

Every customer who comes from an ad goes through the same few steps. At each one, some people drop off. Knowing where helps you fix the right thing.

## 1. They see your ad

This is your ad's job: the picture, the words, the audience. If people are clicking, this part is working.

## 2. They click

Now they land somewhere: a page, a form, or a chat. If it's slow, cluttered or doesn't answer their first question, many leave right away.

**What helps:** one clear next step, and the main answers (what it is, where, how to book) easy to find.

## 3. They send a message

They ask about price, timing or details. This is a person who is interested right now. If the reply takes hours, that interest fades, or they book somewhere else.

**What helps:** fast replies, any time of day, in the language they wrote in.

## 4. They think about it

Plenty of people don't decide on the spot. That's normal. The problem is when nobody gets back to them.

**What helps:** a friendly follow-up the next day.

## 5. They book

Good news, but not the finish line. People forget, or something comes up.

**What helps:** a confirmation right away and a reminder before the appointment.

## 6. They pay

This is the moment you were paying for from the start.

## Where to start

You don't have to fix everything at once. Count how many people make it through each step, find the biggest drop, and start there.
